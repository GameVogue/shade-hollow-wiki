**The Metals**
- *Iron* `(ferrum)` - Absorbs and stores shade energy
- *Gold* `(aurum)` - Repels shade energy, protects against shades
- *Silver* `(argentum)` - Attracts shade energy, conductive
- *Lead* `(satum)` - Impermeable to shade energy, fixative to alchemical properties
- *Copper* `(cuprum)` - Conductive, antimicrobial, impervious to non-oxidizing liquids, commonly used for fittings and vessels in alchemy

**The Solvents**
- *Alcohol* `(ethyl)` - Common solvent, psychoactive depressant, disinfectant
- *Ether* `(ether)` - Common solvent, general anesthetic, analgesic, extremely flammable, smells sweet, hallucinogenic.
- *Alkahest* `(alkahest)` - Hard to create but pure and potent solvent. Lacking any additional effects.
- *Sunwater* `(aqua solus)` - Water evaporation-distilled using sunlight over a gold plated mirror, purified water.
- *Moonwater* `(aqua lunarus)` - A solution of water and alcohol mixed with ground silver.

**The Vitriols**
A Vitriol is an alchemical crystal or powder, also used as pigments in dye and paint.
- *Blue Vitriol* `(cuprum vitre)` - Herbicide/insecticide
- *Red Vitriol* `(realgar vitre)` - Arsenic, rat poison
- *Green Vitriol* `(ferrum vitre)` - Fixative, reducing agent
- *Yellow Vitriol* `(orpiment vitre)` - Arsenic, anti-inflammatory
- *Black Vitriol* `(bitumen vitre)` - Sealant, semi-solid, viscous and sticky black tar.

**The Salts**
- *Saltpetre* `(sal petrae)` - Potassium nitrate, fertilizer, propellant, oxidizer, component of blackpowder
- *Seasalt* `(sal oceanus)` - Sodium chloride, obtained by boiling seawater
