A shade is a spirit or ghost resulting from a human soul retaining its shape instead of dissipating into ambient magical energy. Shades are rare.

### Creating a Shade
Intentionally creating a shade is illegal, both in terms of the law of the Kingdom and in terms of the doctrines of the Church. Such a thing hasn't been publicly prosecuted since the early days of the Church.

Still, several rituals are theorized by scholars, and almost certainly practiced by the most evil sort of shade-weavers in secret.

Even more rarely, a shade can create itself naturally if an incredibly strong willed person dies while holding in their mind an all-consuming singular purpose which holds their soul together.

Shades are thought to be attracted to silver, repelled by gold, and sedated by iron.

### Consciousness and Temperament
Shades are often perceived as angry or scared, as well as stubborn or purposeful. It is not known if they are truly conscious or just a shadow of their living self. Shades are usually unpredictable and difficult to understand, following a sort of dream-logic.

### Abilities and Study
Shades have shown a startling ability to wield magic, often far beyond what a living person can. Additionally, their [[Shade-Weaving]] does not always follow commonly understood rules, and in many cases they seem to alter or warp our perception rather than the physical world itself, which makes them difficult to study. Study itself is only possible in a few remote places where shades are persistent or re-appear frequently.
