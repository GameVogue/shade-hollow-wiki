[[Culture of Aventia]] is, of course, monarchist. The royal family is considered to be an unbroken line, although the crown has passed to cousins, adopted heirs, illegitimate heirs, second cousins, and the like often enough for the line to become quite squiggly.

The current king is mild-tempered and inattentive, while the queen is ardently religious and has yet born no children, though the king is near fifty and the queen in mid thirties. They married by arrangement sixteen years ago after an unsuccessful string of other courtships by the king.

No one is quite certain, at least publicly, what will happen if the pair never bring forth any children, but several cousins are waiting eagerly to find out.
