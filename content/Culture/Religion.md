Gods aren't real, but rituals do have power, and religion is common in the [[Kingdom of Aventia]].

## The Church
The Kingdom has one official religion which serves a binary Sun and Moon god-pair. Stars represent human souls that have not yet fallen down to earth. Shooting stars herald a birth somewhere on the horizon.

In reality, the carefully designed prayer rituals performed in churches and cathedrals across the kingdom actually siphon small amounts of magic from each participant, which can then be used by the officiant abbot or monk to perform a minor act of magic like healing a wound, creating a light, sound, or heat, or causing a small object to become temporarily invisible.

The Abbatry is a devout religious institution deeply embedded in the [[Culture of Aventia#Old Aelmor]]. The High Abbot is almost as influential as any noble, and has the ear of the queen.
#### The Gods
The Sun and Moon are taught either as a man and woman, or as one masculine entity with two faces. This difference is a major divide in the faith - Binarites vs Diunites. Binarism is the more traditional understanding, Diunism is reactionary.

### Folk Religions
It's common for folks on the outskirts, the hard to reach places, and the forgotten backwaters to believe in their own set of deities, spirits, and superstitions. The kingdom does not formally outlaw folk religions, but it does not recognize their holidays or customs either.
