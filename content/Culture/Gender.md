[[Culture of Aventia]] suffers from a moderate affliction of patriarchy, as a secondary knock-on effect of monarchism. Society in general favors men for most roles in leadership, but women can hold leadership roles as long as they work harder than the men around them.

Non-binary and transgender people are closeted except in permissive pocket communities (communes, monasteries, woodland bandit gangs, secret occult circles).

The most accessible path upwards for women is through education. City-born women are often subject matter experts and knowledge workers, especially in the fields of education, medicine, accounting, administration, and scholarship. Country-born women are often craftspeople.

### Cultural stereotypes about women:
*(These are obviously untrue, but of course in some ways self-fulfilling.)*
- Women are smarter than men, but less trustworthy.
- Women's eyes are better attuned to reading, but worse at spotting prey or threat.
- Women's hands are better attuned to precise, dexterous, or repetitive tasks.
- Women are worse at art because they are more calculating and logical, less feeling.
- Women aren't funny and cannot be court fools.

Men are of course the "default" that contrasts these stereotypes.
