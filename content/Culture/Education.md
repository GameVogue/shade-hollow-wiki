Most provinces in [[The Kingdom of Aventia]] practice semi-communal child-rearing and skill apprenticeship. A person learns how to farm, fish, cook, sew, hunt, and the like from their parents, neighbors, and local craftspeople.

In major cities it is common for merchant and noble class people to attend a school and then apply for a college or guild. Many such institutions are paid for by the city itself to increase the availability of skilled services and luxury trade goods, and therefore taxes.
