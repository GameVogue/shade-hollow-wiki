Aventia has been in its current form for about two hundred and fifty years, and has had eleven kings before the current one.

## Cultural Regions
Aventia has three major cultural regions, leftover from pre-Maurovian Era unification. These regions are now broad and fuzzy, with no hard boundaries, although some province lines still follow the old country borders.

#### Old Aventia
*Latin and Spanish language roots*
- The south-eastern coast of the peninsula.
- A mixture of Fisherfolk traditions and Thaspian culture.
- Fishing, sailing, swimming, bathing, salt, medicine, philosophy, expansionism
- What if the ancient Roman state had embraced a Grecian naval culture and a Viking raider style of colonial conquest.
#### Old Kapova
*Slavic and Celtic language roots*
- The western border and south-western coast of the peninsula.
- A mixture of Marshfolk, Sovelkan, and Kapovian cultural traditions.
- Damp, miserable, metalworking, rugged
- What if the Irish and Picts and Scandinavian raiders had peacefully integrated because nothing in the swamp is worth fighting over.
#### Old Aelmor
*Saxon and English language roots*
- The central region and north-eastern coast of the peninsula.
- Aelmorian cultural traditions.
- Farming, herding, baking, beer drinking, fort-building, wheat, religion
- What if England had been eaten by a stronger and less inbred evil empire.
