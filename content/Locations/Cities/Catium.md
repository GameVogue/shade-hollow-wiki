[[Culture of Aventia#Old Aventia]]

**Early History**
The old city of Catium began as a fishing village, of forgotten name, that sprang up in the shelter of what would become Catium Bay, a peaceful inlet shielded by a craggy half-circle of partially submerged rocks. Unfortunately for the Fisherfolk of the region, this bay was where Domarc Avent chose to shelter his fleet of raiding longships, who made quick work of the village's fighting-aged men.

Domarc Avent claimed the bay and village for himself and named it Catium, related to a Thaspian word meaning rare or bright. This small village would provide the foundation for fifty years of conquest, and grew accordingly.

**Cusorian Era**
Cuso Avent declared Catium the capital of Aventia, taking after the customs of nearby nations to declare a stationary seat of power.
