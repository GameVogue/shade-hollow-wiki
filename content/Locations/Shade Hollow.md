[[Culture of Aventia#Old Kapova]]

The Aris Estate, known by the locals as Shade Hollow, is a small stronghold estate located in Corum hold in the province of Vennia. Corum hold is lowland largely comprised of mire and bog, and is divided from the rest of Vennia by a range of low rocky ridges that funnel rain water back into the mire.

The noble family that originally built the estate made their moderate wealth from the iron trade, having discovered a rich deposit of [bog-iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bog_iron), which has since dried up. Now the principle export is [peat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peat), which is used as a fuel source as well as in agriculture.

The house itself is built upon a small hill facing north, backed by bog forest and with a meager farmland to the east along a terraced slope. To the west is open swampland.
